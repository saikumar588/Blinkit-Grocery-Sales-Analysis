# Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit's grocery sales data using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to understand sales performance, identify top-performing product categories, and evaluate outlet performance across different outlet sizes and location tiers.

An interactive three-page dashboard was developed to transform raw grocery sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall grocery sales performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Understand the impact of outlet size and location on sales.
- Compare sales performance across different outlet types.
- Analyze average product ratings and item visibility.
- Identify business patterns and opportunities for improvement.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs.
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset storage and initial data inspection.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The project uses a Blinkit grocery sales dataset containing **8,523 records and 12 columns**.

The dataset includes information about:

- Item Identifier
- Item Weight
- Item Fat Content
- Item Visibility
- Item Type
- Sales
- Rating
- Outlet Identifier
- Outlet Establishment Year
- Outlet Size
- Outlet Location Type
- Outlet Type

## 📈 Key Business Observations

Based on the dashboard:

1. Total sales are approximately 1.20 million.
2. Tier 3 outlets contribute the highest sales among the three location tiers.
3. Supermarket Type 1 contributes the largest share of sales among outlet types.
4. Fruits and Vegetables and Snack Foods are the top-performing item categories.
5. Regular fat-content products contribute a larger share of sales than low-fat products.
6. The average product rating is approximately 3.92.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing product categories such as Fruits and Vegetables and Snack Foods.
- Analyze the strategies used by Tier 3 outlets to understand their stronger sales contribution.
- Maintain product availability in categories generating higher sales.
- Evaluate outlet-level performance to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Monitor product ratings to maintain customer satisfaction.
